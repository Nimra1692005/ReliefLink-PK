THE PROBLEM

Pakistan Faces Devastating Floods Every Year

2022 floods affected 33 million people, killed 1,739, caused $30 billion in damage. No unified platform existed for real-time emergency response.

🌊
No Early Warning
Citizens get no real-time flood predictions or weather alerts before disaster strikes
🏥
Can't Find Help
No single platform to find nearest hospitals, shelters, or safe routes during emergencies
📡
No Coordination
Rescue teams, volunteers, and victims have no unified communication channel
OUR SOLUTION

One Platform. Every Emergency.

ReliefLink combines AI prediction, live weather, real-time maps, SOS alerts, and volunteer coordination — all free, all in one place.

FEATURE 1

Landing Page & Role Selection

Beautiful animated landing page with role-based entry. Users choose between "I Need Help" (victim) or "I Want to Help" (volunteer).

How to use:
1. Open the app URL in any browser
2. Watch the animated volunteer character showcase features
3. Click "Enter as User" for emergency services
4. Click "Enter as Volunteer" to register as helper
Landing Page — Role Selection Interface
Live Operations Map — Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap
FEATURE 2

Live Operations Map

Interactive Leaflet.js map showing real-time locations of hospitals, shelters, pharmacies, rivers, SOS alerts, and risk zones across Pakistan.

How to use:
1. Click 🗺️ Map in navigation bar
2. Toggle layers: SOS, Hospitals, Shelters, Pharmacies, Risk Zones
3. Click 📍 My Location to center on your GPS position
4. Click 📡 Live Track for continuous GPS tracking
5. Zoom & pan to explore any area in Pakistan
FEATURE 3

Weather Intelligence

Live weather data from OpenWeatherMap for all major Pakistan cities. Shows temperature, humidity, wind speed, conditions, and weather icons.

How to use:
1. Click 🌤️ Weather in navigation
2. View live weather cards for Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta, etc.
3. Each card shows: temp (°C), humidity, wind, conditions
4. Data auto-refreshes every 5 minutes
Weather Intelligence — OpenWeatherMap Integration
Nearby Search — OpenStreetMap Overpass API
FEATURE 4

Nearby Hospitals & Shelters

Find the closest hospitals, shelters, and pharmacies using OpenStreetMap's Overpass API. Search by city name or use GPS for instant results.

How to use:
1. Click 📍 Nearby in navigation
2. Type a city/area name (e.g., "Lahore") — autocomplete suggestions appear
3. Or click 📍 GPS to use your current location
4. Or tap a quick city chip: Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, etc.
5. View results: distance, direction, weather at that location
FEATURE 5

🚨 SOS Emergency System

One-tap emergency SOS that captures GPS location, lets you select emergency type (flood, fire, medical, earthquake, etc.), and sends alert to rescue teams.

How to use:
1. Click 🚨 SOS Alert button (top-right) or Quick Action card
2. Select emergency type: 🌊 Flood, 🔥 Fire, 🏥 Medical, 🏚️ Earthquake, ⛰️ Landslide, etc.
3. Click 📍 Get Location to capture GPS coordinates
4. Optionally describe your emergency in the text box
5. Click "Send SOS — Get Help Now"
6. Track your SOS status with real-time timeline
SOS Emergency — GPS-based Rescue Coordination
AI Assistant — Groq-Powered Multilingual Chat
FEATURE 6

🤖 AI Chat Assistant

Multilingual AI chatbot powered by Groq. Ask about weather, floods, emergency procedures, evacuation routes — in English or Roman Urdu.

How to use:
1. Click 💬 floating button (bottom-right) or the AI Hero Card on dashboard
2. Type any question: "Weather in Lahore?", "Flood safety tips", etc.
3. Click suggested quick-reply buttons for common queries
4. AI responds with real-time data from OpenWeatherMap
5. Supports English and Roman Urdu language
FEATURE 7

🧠 AI Flood-Risk Predictor

Machine Learning logistic regression model that predicts flood probability using live rainfall forecasts, humidity, and monsoon-season factors for Pakistan river basins.

How to use:
1. Click 🧠 AI Predictor in navigation
2. Select a location: Islamabad (Nullah Lei), Lahore (Ravi), Karachi (Malir), etc.
3. Click 🧠 Run AI Prediction
4. View probability score (0-100%) with color-coded risk level
5. See 4 ML features: cumulative rainfall, peak 24h rain, humidity, monsoon factor
6. Read AI-generated safety recommendations
AI Flood Predictor — Logistic Regression ML Model
Route Planner — OSRM + Weather Along Route
FEATURE 8

🚗 Safe Route Planner

Plan safe travel routes with real-time weather conditions and disaster alerts along the way. Uses OSRM for routing and OpenWeatherMap for en-route weather.

How to use:
1. Click 🚗 Route in navigation
2. Enter From and To cities (e.g., Lahore → Kasur)
3. Or click a preset route: Islamabad→Murree, Karachi→Hyderabad, etc.
4. Click 🚗 Plan Route
5. View: distance, ETA, weather along route, turn-by-turn directions
6. Click ⇄ to swap origin and destination
FEATURES 9-10

Real-Time Alerts & River Monitoring

🚨
Real-Time Disaster Alerts
Live earthquake data from USGS, global disasters from GDACS, and weather alerts from OpenWeatherMap. Filter by type: earthquakes, floods, storms.
Click 🚨 Alerts → Filter tabs → View severity-coded alert cards
Usage: Navigate to 🚨 Alerts tab. Click filter tabs (All, Earthquakes, Floods, Storms, Weather). Each card shows severity, location, time, and source. Auto-refreshes every 5 min.
🏞️
River Level Monitoring
Tracks 8 major Pakistan rivers (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Sutlej, Kabul, etc.) with color-coded status from Low to Exceptionally High based on FFD thresholds.
Always visible on dashboard → Color-coded river chips
Usage: Visible on the Dashboard home page. Each river chip shows name and status with color: 🔵 Low, 🟢 Normal, 🟡 Medium, 🟠 High, 🔴 Very High.
FEATURES 11-12

Volunteers & Safe Places

🤝
Volunteer Network
Register as volunteer with name, phone, city, availability, and 12 skill categories (first aid, swimming, medical, search & rescue, etc.). View active roster.
Usage: Click 🤝 Volunteers → Fill form → Select availability (Available/Weekends/Evenings/On-call) → Check skills → Click "Register as volunteer". View recently active volunteers on the right.
🏠
Safe Places Finder
Find nearby shelters, mosques, schools, and hospitals as safe zones during emergencies. Live data from OpenStreetMap with distance and status badges.
Usage: Click 🏠 Safe Places → Filter by type (All, Shelters, Hospitals, Mosques, Schools) → Each card shows name, distance, address, and open/full status.
